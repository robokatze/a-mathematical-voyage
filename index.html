Hello and welcome

I’m Jishnu Ghosh, a student at the Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata, with a deep passion for mathematics—especially olympiad-style problem solving and competitive exams. This blog is dedicated to documenting my experiences prepping for contests like RMO, INMO, and IOQM, as well as entrance exams including ISI and CMI. You’ll find topic-wise handouts, curated problem sets, and other essential resources designed to sharpen your skills in algebra, geometry, number theory, combinatorics and analysis. Whether you’re aiming for math competitions or top-tier exam success, I hope these materials and insights make your preparation both effective and enjoyable. Welcome to the journey—I hope you find it helpful and inspiring!
